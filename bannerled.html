BannerLED Privacy Policy

BannerLED helps you create bright LED-style scrolling text banners for display on your Android device. This policy explains what information is handled by the app and how it is used.

App: BannerLED Developer: LTeam Effective date: June 9, 2026 Contact: lteamrick3@gmail.com

Your banner settings stay local

Text, colors, speed, size, direction, font, display preferences, and purchase state are stored on your Android device unless platform backup or sharing is used.

Google services are used

BannerLED uses Google services for ads, ad consent, analytics, crash reports, remote configuration, Play Services, and Google Play Billing.

No BannerLED account is required

BannerLED does not require a BannerLED account and does not operate its own cloud sync service for your banner content.

1. Information You Provide or Create

When you use BannerLED, you may create or choose information such as:

  • Banner text and display content.
  • Text color, background color, border color, LED effect, font, size, speed, spacing, direction, and animation preferences.
  • Saved or restored app preferences, language preference, ad-removal state, subscription state, and related settings.

BannerLED stores this user-created banner content and related preferences in the app's local storage on your Android device. LTeam does not automatically upload your banner text, colors, display settings, or saved preferences to LTeam servers.

2. Local Storage and Backup

BannerLED is designed as a local-first app. Banner content, visual settings, display preferences, and related app preferences are stored locally using Android app storage.

The app currently allows Android backup and uses Android data extraction rules. Depending on your Android version, Google account, device manufacturer, and device settings, Android or Google backup and device-transfer systems may back up or transfer app data. LTeam does not control those platform backup systems.

3. Information Collected Automatically

BannerLED uses Firebase Analytics to understand broad app usage and improve the app. Analytics events may include screen views, ad lifecycle events, purchase actions, display actions, settings changes, and premium state.

BannerLED is designed to avoid sending the exact banner text or exact local settings that you enter. Some analytics values may include general states, product identifiers, plan identifiers, ad placement, and ad value information reported by Google Mobile Ads.

Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash reports and diagnostics such as crash traces, app version, device model, operating system version, timestamps, installation identifiers, premium state, and other technical information needed to diagnose app errors.

Firebase Remote Config may be used to configure app behavior, ads, or feature availability. Remote Config may process technical identifiers and device/app information according to Firebase policies.

4. Advertising, Consent, and Purchases

BannerLED is an ad-supported app and may show app open ads, banner ads, native ads, interstitial ads, or rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and related Google services may collect or receive information such as IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device information, advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals to provide ads, measure performance, prevent abuse, and comply with legal requirements.

BannerLED uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required to request and manage ad privacy choices. Depending on your region and settings, you may be able to choose whether ads are personalized. BannerLED may also provide a privacy options entry point for managing ad consent choices when available.

BannerLED may offer subscriptions or ad-removal purchases through Google Play Billing. Purchase processing is handled by Google Play. LTeam and BannerLED receive purchase status, product identifiers, plan identifiers, purchase tokens, and entitlement state needed to unlock, acknowledge, and restore paid access, but do not receive your full payment card details.

You can manage ad personalization and advertising ID settings through your Android device settings and Google's ad settings.

5. Permissions and Device Features

BannerLED may use the following Android permissions or system features, including permissions added by Google SDKs used by the app:

  • Internet and network state: used for ads, analytics, crash reporting, consent management, Remote Config, Play Billing, and related Google services.
  • Advertising ID: may be used by Google Mobile Ads and related Google services for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and privacy controls where available.
  • Local app storage: used to save banner content, display settings, subscription state, and preferences on your device.

BannerLED does not request camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, SMS, call log, calendar, or account access.

6. Third-Party Services

BannerLED may use the following third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform and AdMob privacy controls
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google Payments Privacy Notice

7. How Information Is Used

Information handled by BannerLED is used to:

  • Provide LED banner creation, scrolling text display, fullscreen display, color selection, font selection, animation, and saved preference features.
  • Show and measure ads, manage ad consent, and support ad-funded app functionality.
  • Process purchases, unlock premium features, remove ads for active paid users, and restore purchases.
  • Understand app usage patterns and improve reliability, usability, performance, and feature quality.
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes or technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests when you choose to contact LTeam.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

LTeam does not sell your banner text, saved banner settings, or local preferences. Information may be shared or processed in these cases:

  • With third-party service providers listed in this policy, as needed to provide ads, analytics, crash reporting, billing, consent management, Remote Config, and related app functionality.
  • With Android, Google backup, or device-transfer services when enabled by your device and account settings.
  • When you intentionally contact support, send screenshots, or otherwise move information outside BannerLED.
  • When required to comply with law, enforce policies, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect rights, safety, and security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Banner content, display settings, and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you edit them, clear app data, uninstall the app, restore or transfer app data, or otherwise remove them through Android or BannerLED features.

Data included in Android backups or device transfers is retained according to Android, Google, manufacturer, and account settings. You can manage backup behavior through your device and Google account settings.

Analytics, crash, ads, consent, Remote Config, and billing data processed by Google services are retained according to those providers' policies and your account/device settings.

Because BannerLED does not require a BannerLED account, there is no BannerLED account to delete. You can delete local BannerLED data by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

10. Security

BannerLED uses Android app storage, Android permission controls, and platform billing/ads SDKs to support app functionality. Data sent to Google services by their SDKs is transmitted using secure transport where provided by those services. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, lock screen, Google account, backups, screenshots, and any external service you choose to use.

11. Children's Privacy

BannerLED is not directed to children under 13. LTeam does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through BannerLED, contact LTeam so appropriate action can be taken.

12. International Use

Third-party services used by BannerLED may process information on servers located in different countries. By using BannerLED, you understand that technical, analytics, advertising, crash, consent, Remote Config, and billing data may be processed outside your country of residence according to the applicable third-party policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

LTeam may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of BannerLED after a policy update means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or BannerLED privacy practices, contact LTeam at lteamrick3@gmail.com.

For faster support, include your app version, Android version, device model, and a short description of your request.
